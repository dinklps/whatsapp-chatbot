# Privacy Policy — WhatsApp Enquiry Assistant

**Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd.** (506942-D)<br>
Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how **Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd.** ("we", "us") handles personal data when you contact
our conveyancing department through our WhatsApp enquiry assistant. It is issued in accordance
with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia ("PDPA").

## 1. What this service is

Our WhatsApp number is answered first by an automated assistant. It asks a few short questions
to understand who you are, which property your enquiry concerns and what you need, and then
passes your enquiry to the staff member in charge of that property. The assistant does not give
legal advice and does not make decisions about your file.

## 2. Personal data we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp, we collect:

- your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
- the messages you send us, and our replies;
- details you provide in the conversation: your name, whether you are a purchaser, lawyer, bank
  officer or other party, your firm or bank, the project name, unit number and location of the
  property, and a description of your enquiry;
- the date and time of each message.

Voice notes, images and documents sent to the assistant are not opened or analysed by it. We
record only that one was sent, so that our staff can follow up with you.

Please do not send identity card numbers, bank account details, passwords or other sensitive
information to the assistant. Our staff will tell you how to provide such information if it is needed.

## 3. Why we use your personal data

We use your personal data to:

- respond to your enquiry and direct it to the right member of staff;
- contact you about your enquiry, including by WhatsApp;
- keep a record of enquiries received about each property;
- handle complaints and improve our service;
- comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

Providing this information is voluntary, but without it we may be unable to identify your file
or respond to your enquiry.

## 4. Automated processing

Your messages are processed by an artificial intelligence service so that the assistant can
understand your enquiry and reply in your language. It is used only to collect the information
described above and to pass your enquiry to our staff. Our staff, not the assistant, deal with
your enquiry itself.

## 5. Who we share your personal data with

We disclose your personal data only as needed for the purposes above, to:

- our staff who handle the property concerned, and their head of department;
- **Meta Platforms, Inc.**, which operates WhatsApp and delivers messages between you and us;
- **Anthropic, PBC**, which provides the artificial intelligence service that processes your
  messages on our behalf;
- our hosting and IT service providers, who store the data on our behalf;
- regulators, courts or authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for marketing by third parties.

## 6. Transfer outside Malaysia

Meta, Anthropic and our hosting providers may process personal data on servers outside Malaysia.
By using this service you consent to that transfer. We use providers that commit to protecting
the data they process for us.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for **seven (7) years** after the enquiry is
closed, or longer where the law requires, and then delete or anonymise them.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised
access, including restricting access to authorised staff and protecting data in transit.
WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own security and privacy terms.

## 9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

- request access to the personal data we hold about you;
- request correction of personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
- withdraw your consent or limit how we process your personal data;
- make an enquiry or complaint about how your personal data is handled.

To do so, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity, and a fee may
be charged for access requests as permitted by law. If you withdraw consent we may be unable to
continue handling your enquiry through WhatsApp.

# Dasar Privasi — Pembantu Pertanyaan WhatsApp

**Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd.** (506942-D)<br>
Kemas kini terakhir: 19 November 2026

Dasar ini menerangkan cara **Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd.** ("kami") mengendalikan data peribadi apabila anda
menghubungi jabatan pindah milik hartanah (conveyancing) kami melalui pembantu pertanyaan WhatsApp.
Dasar ini dikeluarkan selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 ("PDPA").

## 1. Perkhidmatan ini

Nombor WhatsApp kami dijawab terlebih dahulu oleh pembantu automatik. Ia mengemukakan beberapa
soalan ringkas untuk mengetahui siapa anda, hartanah yang berkenaan dan apa yang anda perlukan,
kemudian memajukan pertanyaan anda kepada kakitangan yang bertanggungjawab. Pembantu ini tidak
memberi nasihat undang-undang dan tidak membuat keputusan mengenai fail anda.

## 2. Data peribadi yang dikumpul

- nombor telefon WhatsApp dan nama profil WhatsApp anda;
- mesej yang anda hantar kepada kami dan balasan kami;
- butiran yang anda berikan: nama, sama ada anda pembeli, peguam, pegawai bank atau pihak lain,
  firma atau bank anda, nama projek, nombor unit dan lokasi hartanah, serta keterangan pertanyaan anda;
- tarikh dan masa setiap mesej.

Nota suara, gambar dan dokumen yang dihantar kepada pembantu tidak dibuka atau dianalisis olehnya.
Sila jangan hantar nombor kad pengenalan, butiran akaun bank, kata laluan atau maklumat sensitif
lain kepada pembantu ini.

## 3. Tujuan penggunaan

Untuk menjawab pertanyaan anda dan memajukannya kepada kakitangan yang betul; menghubungi anda
mengenai pertanyaan tersebut; menyimpan rekod pertanyaan bagi setiap hartanah; mengendalikan aduan
dan menambah baik perkhidmatan; serta mematuhi kewajipan undang-undang kami. Pemberian maklumat ini
adalah secara sukarela, tetapi tanpanya kami mungkin tidak dapat menjawab pertanyaan anda.

## 4. Pemprosesan automatik

Mesej anda diproses oleh perkhidmatan kecerdasan buatan supaya pembantu dapat memahami pertanyaan
anda dan membalas dalam bahasa anda. Kakitangan kami, bukan pembantu, yang menguruskan pertanyaan anda.

## 5. Pendedahan kepada pihak lain

Data peribadi anda didedahkan hanya setakat yang perlu kepada: kakitangan kami yang berkenaan dan
ketua jabatan mereka; **Meta Platforms, Inc.** (pengendali WhatsApp); **Anthropic, PBC** (pembekal
perkhidmatan kecerdasan buatan yang memproses mesej bagi pihak kami); pembekal perkhidmatan
pengehosan dan IT kami; serta pihak berkuasa jika dikehendaki oleh undang-undang. Kami tidak menjual
data peribadi anda.

## 6. Pemindahan ke luar Malaysia

Meta, Anthropic dan pembekal pengehosan kami mungkin memproses data peribadi di pelayan di luar
Malaysia. Dengan menggunakan perkhidmatan ini, anda bersetuju dengan pemindahan tersebut.

## 7. Tempoh penyimpanan

Rekod pertanyaan disimpan selama **tujuh (7) tahun** selepas pertanyaan ditutup, atau lebih lama
jika dikehendaki oleh undang-undang, dan kemudian dipadam atau dijadikan tanpa nama.

## 8. Keselamatan

Kami mengambil langkah yang munasabah untuk melindungi data peribadi anda daripada kehilangan,
penyalahgunaan dan akses tanpa kebenaran.

## 9. Hak anda

Di bawah PDPA, anda boleh meminta akses kepada data peribadi anda, meminta pembetulan, menarik balik
persetujuan atau mengehadkan pemprosesan, dan membuat pertanyaan atau aduan. Sila hubungi kami
melalui butiran di bawah. Jika anda menarik balik persetujuan, kami mungkin tidak dapat terus
mengendalikan pertanyaan anda melalui WhatsApp.
